About Voltaire

Voltaire Research is an independent computational research studio exploring intelligence, markets, sound and planetary systems.

The institution operates through a collection of focused research initiatives, each investigating a distinct domain through mathematical modeling, machine learning and experimental software.

Voltaire emphasizes independent inquiry, technical depth, reproducibility and the development of computational systems that extend beyond conventional applications.

Research outputs may take the form of software, experimental models, technical reports, datasets and publications.
